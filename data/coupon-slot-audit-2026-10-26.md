# Dealer-side slot audit, 2026-10-26

Two pages were assigned: crandalloffice and btod. Both were read at their own pages. Both
fail the four-slot gate, so neither shipped and the site was not rebuilt or deployed.

The premise behind this batch was wrong and it is worth writing down: it was assumed that
dealers print the code and the exclusions on the same page as the offer. These two dealers
print no code at all. Crandall's own site search returns "0 results found for coupon", and
BTOD's footer link to its sign-up coupon page (`/subscribe-n-save/`) is a 404 on its own
site.

## Crandall Office Furniture — 2 of 4

Printed, read 2026-10-26:

- Site-wide announcement bar: "Think V1 Chairs Are 15% Off - Click Here to Shop Now". No code
  string, no end date, no terms attached.
- FAQ, shipping: "we are not currently shipping chairs to Alaska, Hawaii, or outside of the
  United States at this time."
- FAQ, returns: 12-year warranty on remanufactured chairs, 30-day return.
- Warranty page: "Limit of One Free Chair Return Per Customer/Household"; chairs with Brisa
  vinyl or leather, or with wear and damage, take a 15% restocking fee.
- Clearance page: "Shop Limited-Time Deals on Steelcase, Herman Miller, and Haworth Office
  Chairs While Supplies Last!"; one listing is marked "CLOSEOUT - NO RETURNS".
- Terms of service: a general errors-and-omissions clause only. No coupon, stacking or
  exclusion language anywhere on the site.

Slots: who cannot use = filled (US only, no AK/HI/international). Which models = filled (the
banner names the V1 Think, and the clearance list names its SKUs). Stacking = empty. End
date = empty; "while supplies last" and "limited-time" are not dates, which is the same call
made on Branch two days ago.

names a scope rather than an exclusion - this is the borderline cell, counted as filled here

## What is missing on both

Stacking. Neither dealer prints a sentence about whether a discount can be combined with
another, because neither runs codes that could be stacked. That is the slot the whole
dealer-side detour ran into, and it is the same wall as the four direct brands, just a
different sentence.

Unlock for each, one line apiece:

- Crandall: a printed end date for the V1 Think 15%, and a sentence on stacking.
- BTOD: a sentence on stacking (the date is already printed, and it is today).
