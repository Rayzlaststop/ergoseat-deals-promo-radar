# Keyword list — 2026-10-17 (deal pages, one keyword per page)

Scope: the 18 providers that have a brand page but no deal page yet
(andaseat, autonomous, branch, btod, crandalloffice, ergodriven, haworth, hbada,
herman-miller, humanscale, ikea, knoll, nouhaus, overstock, sidiz, sihoo, staples, uplift).

The list was empty before this run, so the three checks were run now and stored here.
Method, in order:

1. Demand — Google autocomplete, US/en, run 2026-10-17. Counted how many of the ten
   returned completions are deal-intent (discount, sale, coupon, promo, code, deal).
2. Competition — top ten organic results for the head term, 2026-10-17. Counted how
   many results carry today's price with a capture date. Coupon farms and dated news
   posts score as "no fresh price".
3. Fulfillability — does the brand's own store publish a current price AND a higher
   regular price for the same product on 2026-10-17? A page cannot be built without it.
